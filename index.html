Why does CRISPResso2 overestimate?

CRISPResso2 discards 20-40% of reads during quality filtering. If unedited reads are disproportionately removed, the remaining pool is enriched for edited sequences, inflating the reported efficiency. CRISPRpreserve analyzes every read, producing results that match the Sanger gold standard.
